# 👷 Hard Hat Hero

**Be the General Contractor!** Hard Hat Hero is an Android game where a kid builds a real house, all the way from buying land to a new family moving in.

It's made for smart kids (about 7+). The steps, words, costs and timing are realistic, so it teaches what it really takes to build a house.

## 📲 Download

Get **HardHatHero.apk** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest). Open it on an Android phone or tablet and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.

## 🏗️ What you do

1. **Meet the family.** Learn how many bedrooms and bathrooms they need, what they wish for, their budget, and their move-in date.
2. **Buy land.** Compare 3 lots (town, edge of town, country). Order a survey, soil test and perc test before you buy. Watch out for floodplains!
3. **Design.** Pick one of 3 house plans (with floor plans) that fits the family *and* the budget, then choose the colors.
4. **Get a permit** from the town building department.
5. **Build, step by step (about 45 real steps):** call 811, clear the lot, silt fence, stake the corners, dig, footings with rebar, foundation, curing, waterproofing, backfill, floor joists, wall framing, trusses, sheathing, roofing, house wrap, windows, plumbing, HVAC and electrical rough-in, insulation, drywall, siding, gutters, driveway, paint, cabinets, trim, floors, fixtures, landscaping, cleaning…
6. **Pass inspections** (footing, rough-in, insulation, final), get the Certificate of Occupancy, fix the punch list, and **hand over the keys!**

## 🧠 How it teaches
- **"What's next?"**: before every step the player chooses the correct next step. Wrong answers get a friendly explanation.
- **Hiring subcontractors**: three bids per trade trade off price, quality, speed and reliability.
- **Material takeoffs**: real quantities (cubic yards of concrete, studs, sheets of OSB, bundles of shingles…) at realistic prices.
- **Weather and calendar**: rain, snow and cold stop outdoor work, concrete can't be poured below 40°F, and crews rest on weekends.
- **Random events**: a giant rock while digging, change orders, stolen wire, safety checks, and more.
- **Cut-away view** (🔍 See inside): look inside the walls to see pipes, wires, ducts and insulation, and under the ground to see footings, the frost line, and utility lines.
- **Builder's Handbook**: 90+ builder words with kid-friendly definitions.
- **Read aloud**: 🔊 buttons read the text out loud with the phone's voice.

Every game is **procedurally generated**: a new family, town, climate, land, house plans, crews and weather.

## Building it yourself
The game is plain HTML/JS in `app/src/main/assets/www` (you can open `index.html` in a browser). GitHub Actions builds the signed APK on every push and publishes a release for `v*` tags.
